# HR Leave Policy (India)
Casual Leave (CL): Employees are entitled to 15 days of casual leave per calendar year. Unused CL does not carry forward.
Earned Leave (EL): 18 days per calendar year. EL can carry forward up to a maximum of 45 days.
Sick Leave (SL): 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 days or more.
Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per applicable law. Additional benefits may apply per HR approval.
Paternity Leave: 10 days within 3 months from the child’s birth.
Public Holidays: As per the annually published holiday list for the employee’s location.

# Work From Home (WFH) Policy
WFH Eligibility: Employees may request WFH for up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core Hours: 10:00–17:00 local time; employees are expected to be reachable and online.
Equipment: Company-issued laptop and VPN access are mandatory for WFH days.
Security: Confidential data must not be printed at home. Use only company-approved cloud storage.
Ergonomics: Employees are advised to maintain a safe and ergonomic home setup.

# IT Acceptable Use Policy
Email: Company email is for business use. Do not send confidential files to personal accounts.
Passwords: Use unique passwords and rotate every 90 days. MFA is mandatory for all remote access.
Software: Install only IT-approved software. Requests via Service Desk.
Devices: Only company-managed devices may connect to internal systems. Jailbroken/rooted devices are prohibited.
Data Transfer: Use the secure file gateway for large data transfers; external USBs are disabled by default.

# Code of Conduct
Anti-Harassment: Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Report incidents to HR or the Ethics hotline.
Conflicts of Interest: Disclose any potential conflicts to your manager and Compliance.
Gifts & Entertainment: Gifts exceeding INR 5,000 require pre-approval from Compliance.
Confidentiality: Do not disclose non-public information to external parties without authorization.
Social Media: Employees must not represent company positions without Communications approval.

# Travel & Expense Policy
Bookings: Use the approved corporate travel portal for flights and hotels.
Limits: Domestic hotel bookings capped at INR 8,000 per night (taxes extra).
Meals: Reimbursable with itemized receipts; alcohol is not reimbursable.
Taxis: Prefer app-based, receipt-required. Airport transfers should use economy options.
International Travel: Requires Director approval; obtain insurance before travel.

# Security & Privacy
PII Handling: Store personal data only in approved systems. Do not export PII to spreadsheets unless authorized.
Incident Reporting: Report suspected security incidents within 2 hours to Security Operations.
Data Retention: Follow the Records Retention Schedule; delete data past retention unless litigation hold applies.
Vendor Access: Third-party access requires NDA and least-privilege accounts with expiry dates.

# Regional Variations (APAC)
Public Holidays: Follow the APAC holiday calendar when stationed in those countries.
Expense Currency: Expenses must be reported in local currency with exchange rate proof where required.
